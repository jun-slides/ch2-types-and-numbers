# What We're Learning

<v-clicks>

- Tell apart `str`, `int`, and `float`
- Convert types with `int()`, `float()`, and `str()`
- Do math with `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `//`, `%`, and `**`
- Use PEMDAS in expressions
- Fix errors from mixing text and numbers
- Format decimals with f-strings, like `:.2f`
- Use `round()`, `min()`, and `max()`
